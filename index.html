Prototype · step 1 of the project

Leitmotif

Six D&D characters, six themes, generated in your browser from nothing but the character sheet. No interface yet, no accounts, no polish — this page exists to answer one question: can you hear the difference?

Play them one after another. Each theme runs about 40 seconds and is the same every time you press play.

What is actually happening

Every field of the sheet owns a different musical axis, so that changing one of them is always audible: class chooses the instruments, race sets rhythm and register, alignment picks the mode and the harmonic tension, traits shape how the melody moves, looks shape the timbre and the room.

The sound is synthesised from scratch — no samples, no files, no server. The same character always produces the same theme, which is what will let a shared link play the tune the sender heard.
