Prototype · step 1 of the project

Leitmotif

Six D&D characters, six themes, generated in your browser from nothing but the character sheet. No interface yet, no accounts, no polish — this page exists to answer one question: can you hear the difference?

Play them one after another. Each theme runs about 40 seconds and is the same every time you press play.

What is actually happening

A theme is built from two things. The class supplies a motif — a handful of intervals that is stated, answered and brought back rather than re-invented every bar. The race supplies a bar of rhythm, drawn under each card above, and the melody, the bass and the drums all take their onsets from it, which is what makes them sound like one band.

Around those: alignment picks the mode and how much tension the harmony carries, a second class forks off the motif in another voice, traits thin or fill the rhythm and widen or narrow the motif's reach, and looks set the register, the timbre and the size of the room. Nothing is allowed to fight the pulse.

Four phrases, arranged so the first one returns as the last. The sound is synthesised from scratch — no samples, no files, no server — and the same character always produces the same theme, which is what will let a shared link play the tune the sender heard.
